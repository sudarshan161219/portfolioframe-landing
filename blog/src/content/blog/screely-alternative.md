Today, the best options include **Portfolio Frame** (for instant client-side tab capture and privacy), **Shots.so** (for 3D framing), and **BrandBird** (for social marketing visuals).

Before jumping into the comparison, a quick clarification: Screely is a screenshot styling web app. *Screenly* is enterprise digital signage software. Review directories frequently confuse the two names, but this guide is strictly about screenshot mockup utilities. ## Why Look for a Screely Alternative?

Screely pioneered a clean aesthetic: drop an image in, wrap it in a minimalist macOS browser frame, pick a background color, and download. But screenshot workflows have changed, and the original tool has fallen behind in four distinct ways.

### 1. Domain Downtime and Abandonment
The biggest trigger for users switching away has been basic reliability. The original `screely.com` domain suffered repeated outages and SSL lapses. When you need to ship a product update or post a client case study, you cannot afford a 404 error on your daily utility site.

### 2. Rigid Framing Options
Screely only gave you one visual shell: a generic desktop browser window with macOS traffic lights. Modern product demos need variety. If you are showcasing a mobile app, you need realistic mobile wrappers. If you are showing terminal commands, you need a dark command-line frame. Screely never added support for iPhone frames, laptop shells, or frameless drop shadows.

### 3. No Annotation or Redaction Tools
Taking a screenshot is rarely the final step. Most of the time, you need to point out a feature, highlight a button, or blur sensitive data like email addresses, revenue figures, or API keys. Screely offered zero markup features. If you wanted to hide confidential data, you had to edit the file in an external photo editor first. For safe ways to protect client data, read our guide on [how to blur sensitive info in screenshots securely](https://portfolioframe.com/blog/how-to-blur-sensitive-info-in-screenshots).

### 4. Rigid Aspect Ratios and Export Formats
Screely centered your image and added uniform padding around it. It lacked preset aspect ratios for social feeds (such as 16:9 for X/Twitter, 1:1 for LinkedIn, or 9:16 for vertical stories). You also could not export high-DPI assets or transparent PNGs reliably for use inside Figma or slide decks.

## What Makes a Great Screenshot Beautifier?

When testing modern tools to find the best screely alternative, evaluate them against these four technical standards:

* **Local vs. Cloud Processing:** When you upload a dashboard capture to a third-party server, you risk exposing customer PII or private business metrics. Client-side tools render everything inside your browser tab using the HTML5 Canvas API without uploading files anywhere.
* **Capture-to-Edit Workflow:** Traditional tools force you to save a screenshot to your desktop, open a website, drag the file in, edit it, and download it again. Extensions eliminate this friction by capturing your active browser tab directly into the canvas.
* **Canvas Presets and DPI Scaling:** A solid tool lets you switch between 16:9, 1:1, and 4:5 ratios instantly. It should also support 2x and 4x Retina scaling so your text stays crisp on high-resolution displays.
* **Versatility Beyond Screenshots:** The best utilities handle more than static site grabs. They format code snippets with syntax highlighting, style social posts, and provide realistic device shells.

## The 8 Best Screely Alternatives Reviewed

### 1. Portfolio Frame (Best Overall for Privacy and Tab Capture)

I built Portfolio Frame because I was tired of two things: clunky upload workflows and subscription fatigue for basic developer utilities. It runs as both a Chrome extension and a standalone [Portfolio Frame web app](https://app.portfolioframe.com/).

![TODO: Portfolio Frame editor showing tab capture and mockup framing options](/blog/images/TODO.png)

Unlike traditional web apps that send your screenshots to a server, Portfolio Frame operates entirely client-side. Your images and dashboard data never leave your browser, making it completely safe for internal metrics and confidential client screens.

**Key Features:**
* **Instant Capture Modes:** Capture your active browser tab, grab a specific page element, paste code snippets, or upload existing image files.
* **Device and Browser Frames:** Choose from authentic macOS Safari/Chrome browser shells, terminal windows, MacBook laptops, and iPhone mockups, complete with realistic 3D tilt options.
* **Built-in Annotations:** Add arrows, numbered workflow steps, text boxes, highlights, stickers, and brand watermarks (text, logo, or QR code).
* **Dedicated Code Styler:** Beautify code with syntax themes including Dracula, Synthwave '84, Monokai, and GitHub Dark, along with custom language badges.

**Pricing:** Free plan with full core features. Pro is a single **$19 one-time payment** (no recurring subscription) that unlocks SVG exports, glassmorphism effects, batch ZIP exports across all aspect ratios, and the sensitive data redaction tool.

### 2. Shots.so (Best for 3D Presentation Mockups)

Shots.so is a browser-based mockup studio focused on modern presentation layouts. It has become a favorite among UI designers posting their work to Dribbble and Twitter.

**Key Features:**
* Smooth 3D perspective tilts and angle adjustments.
* Multi-device scenes that place an iPhone mockup next to a desktop browser frame.
* Rich library of soft gradients and custom background styling.

**Trade-offs:** Shots.so does not include a browser extension for direct page capture. You must manually take your screenshot, save it locally, and upload it to their web canvas. It also lacks annotation arrows and step indicators.

### 3. BrandBird (Best for SaaS Marketing Teams)

BrandBird is built specifically for SaaS founders and content marketers who post product updates regularly on social feeds. As noted on [Clickcast's beautifier comparison](https://www.clickcast.tech/blog/clickcast-vs-screely-best-screenshot-beautifier), marketing-focused tools emphasize branded presets over simple cropping.

**Key Features:**
* Automated aspect ratio cropping for Twitter, LinkedIn, and Product Hunt.
* 3D transforms, background patterns, and annotation callouts.
* Reusable brand kits with saved colors and watermarks.

**Trade-offs:** BrandBird operates on a recurring SaaS subscription model (starting around $11 to $19 per month). If you only need occasional screenshots, a monthly fee can be hard to justify.

### 4. Pika.style (Best for Quick Web Mockups with Gradients)

Pika is a clean, browser-based image editor that lets you wrap screenshots in simple browser shells and gradient backgrounds.

**Key Features:**
* Fast image adjustments with rounded corners, drop shadows, and padding.
* Built-in text labels, watermarks, and quick blur functionality.
* Copy-to-clipboard button for instant pasting into Slack or documentation.

**Trade-offs:** While Pika offers a free tier, high-resolution exports and advanced framing controls require a recurring monthly subscription.

### 5. Shotcandy (Best Free, Open-Source Alternative)

If you want a bare-bones, free alternative that stays close to Screely's original minimalism, [Shotcandy](https://shotcandy.app/alternatives/) is an open-source tool worth testing. According to community roundups on [FramedShot's screely alternative guide](https://framed-shot.com/screely-alternative/), lightweight open-source utilities fill a crucial gap for cost-conscious creators.

**Key Features:**
* Runs client-side in your browser for solid data privacy.
* Simple, uncluttered interface that wraps images in clean frames.
* Supports batch processing for basic multi-image layouts.

**Trade-offs:** There are no built-in annotation tools, no device wrappers like phones or laptops, and limited options for canvas aspect ratio presets.

### 6. Browser Frame (Best for Exact Browser Chrome Skins)

Browser Frame is a specialized utility built for a single task: wrapping screenshots or URLs into exact replicas of various web browsers.

**Key Features:**
* Authentic UI frames for Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Safari, Microsoft Edge, and legacy systems.
* Can fetch a live URL automatically to generate a preview frame.
* No forced backgrounds or heavy drop shadows.

**Trade-offs:** Browser Frame is not a canvas design studio. You cannot add gradient backdrops, 3D tilts, marketing text, or annotations. It is intended strictly for documentation where exact browser chrome is required.

### 7. Ray.so (Best for Code Snippets)

Ray.so (created by the Raycast team) set the visual benchmark for turning raw code into clean macOS-styled cards. If you were using Screely to display code snippets, Ray.so is a much better specialized alternative.

**Key Features:**
* Beautiful syntax highlighting across dozens of languages.
* Toggleable dark/light modes and adjustable padding.
* Direct SVG and PNG export.

**Trade-offs:** Ray.so only processes text code. It cannot frame web applications, browser windows, or standard visual screenshots.

### 8. CleanShot X (Best Desktop Suite for macOS)

CleanShot X is not a web tool; it is a native macOS application that completely replaces the default Apple screenshot utility (`Cmd + Shift + 4`).

**Key Features:**
* Scrolling screenshot capture, screen recording, and native OCR text recognition.
* Integrated background beautifier with adjustable shadows and padding.
* Comprehensive annotation suite with arrows, numbered badges, and pixelated redaction.

**Trade-offs:** CleanShot X is exclusive to macOS and requires installing desktop software. Pricing starts at a $29 one-time purchase with renewal fees for updates, or an $8/month cloud subscription.

## How to Choose the Right Screely Alternative for Your Workflow

To find the tool that fits your routine, match your choice to your primary constraint:

* **If your priority is privacy and client data:** Pick an in-browser, client-side tool like **Portfolio Frame** or **Shotcandy**. These tools process pixels locally in your browser, keeping client records and private dashboards off third-party servers.
* **If your priority is speed:** Choose an extension or desktop application to bypass the save-and-upload loop. The **Portfolio Frame Chrome extension** lets you capture directly from your browser tab, while **CleanShot X** works directly via global Mac shortcuts.
* **If your priority is mobile UI:** Use **Portfolio Frame** or **Shots.so**. Both support realistic mobile shells. To see how mobile wrappers work in practice, read our guide on [how to create an iPhone screenshot mockup](https://portfolioframe.com/blog/iphone-screenshot-mockup).
* **If your priority is social engagement:** Choose **BrandBird** or **Shots.so** for preset 16:9 social canvases, multi-device stacks, and perspective tilts.

## Final Verdict

Screely made screenshot beautification popular, but its lack of ongoing updates and frequent downtime make modern alternatives necessary. For a native Mac-only desktop workflow, CleanShot X is an excellent utility. For 3D portfolio presentations, Shots.so delivers great results.
